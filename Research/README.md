# Research

Store all research materials related to the project.

## Contents

- Research Papers
- Literature Survey
- Journal Articles
- Conference Papers
- Patent References
- Useful Websites
- Technical Notes

## Naming Convention

Author_Year_Title.pdf

Example:

IEEE_2025_AI_Healthcare.pdf

## Notes

- Prefer IEEE, Springer, Elsevier, ACM, Nature and arXiv references.
- Mention the source for every research paper.
- Keep a summary of important findings.
- Maintain proper citations while using published work.
